About the FamousFace Art Quiz

The FamousFace Art Quiz explores famous artists, paintings, sculptures and important works of art. It is designed for anyone who enjoys learning about visual art and testing their knowledge of well-known artists and masterpieces.

The questions can take you across different periods and artistic traditions, from celebrated painters and iconic artworks to interesting facts about the history of art. You don't need to be an art expert to enjoy the challenge.
