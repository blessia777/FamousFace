Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. About FamousFace

FamousFace is an online quiz platform that allows visitors to test their knowledge of famous people, music, movies, sports, art and history.

2. Information We Collect

When you participate in a FamousFace quiz and choose to submit your score to the leaderboard, we may store information such as your player name, quiz category, score, percentage, total questions and the date and time associated with the score.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information as your player name.

3. Leaderboard

Leaderboard information may be displayed publicly so that visitors can compare quiz results.

Do not submit your full name, telephone number, email address, password or other sensitive information as your player name.

4. Local Storage and Browser Data

FamousFace may use browser local storage to provide features such as quiz history, player progress, achievements, daily challenges and daily streaks.

This information may remain stored in your browser until it is cleared by you or removed by the website's functionality.

5. Google Analytics

FamousFace uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to improve the platform.

Google Analytics may collect information such as page views, interactions, events, device information and general technical information about website usage.

FamousFace uses Analytics events to understand activities such as starting quizzes, completing quizzes, completing Daily Challenges, sharing scores and unlocking achievements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

FamousFace and third-party services may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics and other services.

You can manage or delete cookies and locally stored information through your browser settings. Some website features may not function as intended if certain technologies are disabled.

7. Advertising

FamousFace may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to the applicable policies and settings of those providers.

FamousFace does not sell personal information directly to advertisers.

8. News Content

FamousFace displays news content obtained through third-party news services. News articles may include titles, descriptions, images, publication sources and links to external websites.

When you click a news article, you may be taken to a third-party website. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Third-Party Services

FamousFace uses third-party technology services to provide features such as leaderboard storage, analytics and delivery of news content.

These services may process technical information necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

10. Data Retention and Your Choices

Information submitted to the public leaderboard may remain available for as long as the leaderboard feature requires it.

If you have questions about information associated with your leaderboard entry or wish to request assistance regarding your information, please contact FamousFace using the email address provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

FamousFace provides general entertainment and educational quiz content. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from visitors.

Visitors should not submit sensitive personal information through the quiz, leaderboard, contact forms or other website features.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as FamousFace develops new features or services. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
