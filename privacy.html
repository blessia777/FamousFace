Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. About FamousFace

FamousFace is an online quiz platform that allows visitors to test their knowledge of famous people, music, movies, sports, art and history.

2. Information We Collect

When you participate in a FamousFace quiz and choose to submit your score to the leaderboard, we may store the player name, quiz category, score, percentage, total questions and the date and time associated with the score.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information as your player name.

3. Leaderboard

Leaderboard information may be displayed publicly so that visitors can compare quiz results.

Do not submit your full name, telephone number, email address, password or other sensitive information as your player name.

4. News Content

FamousFace displays news content obtained through third-party news services. News articles may include titles, descriptions, images, publication sources and links to external websites.

When you click a news article, you may be taken to a third-party website. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Contact

If you contact FamousFace by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Contact email: famousfaceba@gmail.com

6. Third-Party Services

FamousFace uses third-party technology services to provide features such as leaderboard storage and delivery of news content.

These services may process technical information necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

7. Children's Privacy

FamousFace is intended to provide general entertainment and educational quiz content. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from visitors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as FamousFace develops new features or services. Updates will be posted on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
